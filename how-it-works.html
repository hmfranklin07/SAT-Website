How it works

A clear path from diagnosis to weekly progress

We start with what your scores actually show—then build a plan you can stick with.

  1. Take a practice test

    You’ll complete a full practice SAT so we have a real baseline— not guesses about where you stand.

  2. Score review & diagnosis

    I look closely at your results to pinpoint what needs the most work—section by section, skill by skill.

  3. Pre-consultation form

    Before we meet, you’ll fill out a short form about what you want to focus on, what’s comfortable, and what your timeline looks like.

  4. Initial consultation

    A discounted first appointment to go over goals, timeline, and scheduling—and for us to get to know each other.

  5. Book and begin

    Then we lock in a rhythm—weekly, twice a week, or whatever fits— and start the work that moves your score.

Ready for step one? Reach out and we’ll get you started.
