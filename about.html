A more intentional path to a better SAT

General prep isn’t the goal here. The focus is a personalized track that meets you where you are—and moves you forward with purpose.

I’m looking to give students a personalized and intentional track to improving on the SAT—not a generic drill packet or a one-size-fits-all syllabus.

That means understanding how you think, what holds your score back, and what a realistic timeline looks like for you. Sessions stay welcoming and clear, with structure that adapts as you grow.

Whether you’re starting from scratch or sharpening an already strong score, the work is built around your needs and your goals.
