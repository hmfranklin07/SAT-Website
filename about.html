About

A more intentional path to a better SAT

Hi, I’m Hannah Franklin, a student at Princeton University with a passion for teaching and helping students build confidence in their academic abilities.

I created this tutoring service because I believe traditional SAT preparation falls short. Test prep is often treated as one-size-fits-all, even though every student learns differently, has different strengths, and struggles with different parts of the test.

What I offer

Personalized, one-on-one SAT instruction

Rather than following the same curriculum for everyone, I focus on identifying where each student can improve most and building a study plan around their individual needs.

  • Strengthening specific concepts
  • Developing better testing strategies
  • Improving timing
  • Approaching question types more effectively

Why personalized tutoring?

Every learner is unique, but most SAT preparation is not.

Large classes, standardized programs, and generic study plans can be helpful, but they often spend too much time on material a student already understands and not enough time addressing the areas holding them back.

I make every tutoring session personalized and intentional. By understanding each student’s strengths, weaknesses, habits, and goals, I can focus our time where it will have the greatest impact.

Ultimately, I want to help students reach their full potential on the SAT while becoming more confident and thoughtful test takers along the way.
